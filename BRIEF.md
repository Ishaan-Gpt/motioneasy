# BRIEF — CapsEasy launch film

## Positioning (owner, 2026-10-07: this overrides every older line)

CaptionsEasy is **cinematic, After Effects-style animated captions that make videos go viral, with no watermark,
completely free and open source**. It is the free, open-source alternative to Submagic. You don't need CapCut Pro.
It gets **Hinglish right**.

**Never position it as "automatic captions".** The whole internet already auto-captions; that line is weak and is banned
everywhere (hooks, claims, end cards, component defaults). Speed, transcription and Whisper are plumbing. Don't lead
with them, and don't put them in a hook or a tagline.

What we sell, in priority order:
1. **Cinematic animated captions**: After Effects-style motion, viral looks, catchy rather than generic white subtitles.
2. **No watermark. Free. Open source.**
3. **The alternative**: Submagic quality without the subscription; no CapCut Pro needed.
4. **Hinglish captions that are actually accurate** (a real gap for Indian creators).
5. Secondary only: 30+ looks, every word in sync, your clips stay on your machine.

Copy rules: catchy and creator-native (a Hinglish line is welcome where it fits), never generic SaaS speak. Competitors are
named in text only (never their logos or UI). Never promise results ("will make you viral"); sell the look ("captions made
to go viral", "viral-style").
