# BRIEF — CapsEasy launch film

## Product (verified facts only)

CapsEasy is a free, open-source, local-first AI video captioning tool.

- Upload a clip in the browser. Whisper transcribes it on your own machine (in-browser or companion app). No cloud needed.
- Pick from 33 caption looks. Style the frame, time every word.
- Export MP4 in the browser, or full renders plus alpha overlays via the companion.
- Footage never leaves your machine. No quotas, no paywall creep.

## The one message

**Stop timing captions. Start posting.**

## Campaign palette (non-negotiable)

- Cream `#ffffeb` — dominant surface, always
- Ink `#1a1a1a` — type and line work
- Lavender `#f0d7ff` — accents, CTAs
- Deep green `#034f46` — closing panels, emphasis

## Quality laws

- No AI slop: no generic gradients, no floating particles, no filler copy, no robotic voiceover.
- Sharp, human copy. Every second earns its place.
- Zero overlapping elements. Phone-safe margins on vertical cuts.
- Real product footage composited in. Nothing faked.

## Deliverables

- Master launch film, 60-90s, 1920x1080
- 15s cutdown + 6s bumper, 1920x1080 and 1080x1920
- Looping backgrounds for social
- Poster/thumbnail frames pulled from hero moments
- Finished MP4s ship via GitHub Releases, never committed to git
