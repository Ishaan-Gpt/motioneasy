# TREATMENT — CapsEasy "Operation: Caption" (60s Spy Title Sequence)

Style: 60s spy title sequence, adapted to the creamprint campaign palette
(warm cream `#ffffeb` dominant, ink `#1a1a1a` silhouettes, lavender `#f0d7ff`,
deep green `#034f46`). 17 seconds, 1080×1920, 24fps. No voice; caption
strips carry the lines, score and foley carry the rhythm.

Logline: a flat-brimmed villain steals the captions; our agent chases him
through a title sequence built from the product's own credits — and the
payoff is the hook: stop timing captions. start posting.

## Structure

- **The briefing (0–2.2s).** An ink circle with a deep-green keyhole blooms
  with a back-ease — an extreme close-up of the title's final dot, returned
  at the end. "THE BRIEFING." Strip: "your mission: caption the video."
- **The theft (2.2–5.8s).** A brass stab cuts in (stab-cut: 80ms cream
  flash). The credit "STARRING · THE UPLOAD" is the set — giant re-cut type
  the villain runs in front of, carrying the glowing lavender "cc" tile,
  our agent in pursuit. Strip: "it stole the captions. get them back."
- **The pursuit (5.8–9.8s).** Two slapstick gags, typography as environment:
  the agent hides behind the "I" of "THE TRANSCRIPTION" while the villain
  passes — the green tie gives him away; then "13 TEMPLATES" becomes a train
  of carriages the agent leaps across in three eased parabolic jumps.
- **The catch (9.8–12.4s).** Graphic match cut: the tile's circle blooms into
  a lavender moon. The tile flies to the agent; at 11.0 the film freezes for
  two beats of true stop-time silence (frozen frame border, "— stop-time —"),
  then resumes as the agent holds the tile high.
- **The title (12.4–15.2s).** The world shatters along the diagonal into six
  sliding strips; "CAPSEASY" assembles letter by letter on 16th-notes at
  132 BPM, each glyph flying in along the diagonal with back-ease landings
  and paper slaps, locking with a click. The keyhole returns beneath it:
  "A CAPSEASY PICTURE."
- **The button (15.2–17.0s).** Quiet. "stop timing captions. start posting."
  lands word by word; a lone bongo answers. Strip: "free · open source ·
  local-first."

## Motion

No linear moves, no hard cuts. Ease-in-out carries, cubic runs, back-ease
slaps and blooms, stepped on-twos run cycles, eased parabolic leaps. Four
designed transitions: stab-cut (flash), graphic match cut (circle), diagonal
shatter wipe, cream dip.

## Sound

"Creamprint Theme," 132 BPM: walking bass, 8th hats, brass stabs landing the
cuts, surf twang on the offbeats, bongo chase, title swell, and foley
throughout (paper slaps, tape clicks, twangs, whooshes, the tile lock, the
paper tear). The stop-time freeze is mixed as genuine silence.

## Claims

Only verified CapsEasy facts: upload a clip; Whisper transcription via the
user's own Groq key; 13 templates; free, open source, local-first. The chase
is a metaphor; nothing in it is a product claim.
