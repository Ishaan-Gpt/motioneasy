# TREATMENT — CapsEasy "Fig. 1 — The Caption Engine" (Blueprint)

Style: Blueprint / engineering drawing, adapted to the creamprint campaign
palette (warm cream `#ffffeb` dominant, ink `#1a1a1a`, lavender `#f0d7ff`,
deep green `#034f46`). 16 seconds, 1080×1920, 24fps. No voice; score and
foley carry the rhythm.

Logline: CapsEasy's local pipeline is presented as an engineering drawing
being drafted in real time — the sheet draws itself, the machine runs, and
the one part that leaves the drawing is the word "posting."

## Structure

- **Sheet (0–2.4s).** A cream drawing sheet slides in; the border draws itself
  with a back-eased pencil run, then a deep-green inspection seal stamps
  "0 BYTES UPLOADED" and the camera punches in through the border on a
  16th-note grid.
- **Drafting order (2.4–7.0s).** Per blueprint grammar (centre lines, then
  outlines, then detail, then dimensions), four pipeline stations draw
  themselves in drafting order: 01 UPLOAD (your clip, straight in),
  02 TRANSCRIBE (Whisper, your own Groq key), 03 STYLE (13 cinematic
  templates), 04 RENDER (1080×1920, this machine). Marching dashed centre
  lines carry the flow downward; the section ends on a dimensioned callout:
  "FOOTAGE TRAVELS 0 mm — STAYS ON THIS MACHINE."
- **In operation (7.0–10.8s).** The stations collapse to a dimmed stack and
  the machine runs: the words "stop / timing / captions." ride a pipe into a
  caption tile, which then explodes into its three spec layers
  (TEXT / TIME / STYLE) with a springy reassembly snap.
- **The magic beat (10.8–13.0s).** Score drops to brushes and shimmer. The
  word "posting." lifts off the drawing as a solid ink object with a shadow
  and a lavender glow — the one part that leaves the drawing — then an iris
  of ink closes and reopens on the end card.
- **Title block (13.0–16.0s).** CAPSEASY, the rule, "free · open source ·
  local-first," the seal stamped again as proof, and the title-block corner
  with "scale 1:1 · sheet 1 of 1." A cream dip closes the film.

## Motion

Every move is eased (ease-in-out carries, cubic pencil runs, back-ease stamps
and pops, spring reassembly). Every scene change is a designed transition:
ruler wipe, punch/scale match, iris, cream dip. Nothing cuts hard; nothing
moves linearly.

## Sound

"Drafting Room Groove," 98 BPM: brushed kit, walking bass, comping guitar,
a breathy lead over the run, shimmer in the magic beat, and foley throughout
(paper slides, drafting scratches, ticks, snaps, whooshes, the seal stamp,
the punch impact, the approval lock).

## Claims

Only verified CapsEasy facts: free, open source, local-first; upload a clip;
