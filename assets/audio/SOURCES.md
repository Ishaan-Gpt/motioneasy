# Audio sources: where to get top-tier SFX and music (free)

Researched October 2, 2026. Everything here is free for commercial use.
The one rule that matters is below. Read it before downloading anything.

## The license rule everyone gets wrong

"Free for commercial use" does NOT mean "safe to commit to a public repo."
Most free-audio licenses allow using the sound **in your videos** but forbid
redistributing the **raw file itself** (which is what a public GitHub repo does).

- **Repo-safe (committed here):** CC0 (Kenney, Freesound CC0-filtered) and
  CC-BY with written attribution (Kevin MacLeod / Incompetech). Both licenses
  explicitly permit redistribution.
- **Use in renders only, never commit raw:** Pixabay, Mixkit, Sonniss GDC
  bundle, ZapSplat free tier. Download them to your own disk, use them in
  films, do not push the files to GitHub.

When in doubt, check the license on the individual file's page. Sites change terms.

## SFX: where to get more

**Tier 1 — download freely, use anywhere, no credit needed:**

- **Pixabay Sound Effects** (pixabay.com/sound-effects) — the pragmatic first
  stop. Large, consistent quality, no attribution. Search: whoosh, riser,
  impact, pop, click, typewriter, success. MP3.
- **Freesound** (freesound.org) — the deepest library on the internet for
  specific, unusual sounds. Filter license to **Creative Commons 0** before
  browsing; anything else needs attribution or is off-limits. CC0 picks are
  repo-safe too. Formats vary (often WAV/FLAC, best quality here).
- **Sonniss GDC Audio Bundle** (sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc) — the pro move.
  Released free every year since 2013, 30GB+ of studio-grade SFX, commercial
  use allowed, no attribution. Download the bundle to local disk, never commit
  it. This is where film-quality whooshes, impacts, and ambiences live.

**Tier 2 — free with conditions:**

- **Mixkit SFX** (mixkit.co/free-sound-effects) — clean and curated, no
  account needed, WAV downloads. Commercial use OK, redistribution banned.
- **ZapSplat** (zapsplat.com) — huge, well-tagged, fast search. Free tier
  requires attribution and bans redistribution. Good when you know exactly
  what you need.
- **YouTube Audio Library** (studio.youtube.com → Audio Library) — zero
  licensing worry for YouTube uploads. Needs a Google account. Check terms
  per track if the film also ships to X/Instagram.

**SFX shopping list for launch videos** (grab one great version of each and
stop): soft whoosh, deep whoosh, riser (2s and 4s), impact hit, pop/click
set, keyboard typing loop, success chime, paper/texture swipe, ambient bed.

## Music: where to get more

**Tier 1 — download freely, use anywhere, no credit needed:**

- **Pixabay Music** (pixabay.com/music) — the best free music pick overall.
  100k+ tracks, searchable by genre/mood/duration/BPM, no attribution, MP3.
  For Apple-style launch films search: corporate, cinematic, minimal,
  uplifting, inspirational. Preview the full track before committing.
- **YouTube Audio Library** — clearly labeled attribution/no-attribution,
  tagged by mood and duration. Safe for YouTube; verify per-track for other
  platforms.

**Tier 2 — free with conditions:**

- **Mixkit Music** (mixkit.co/free-stock-music) — curated, higher average
  quality than Pixabay, no account. License covers online video use.
- **Uppbeat** (uppbeat.io) — hand-picked catalog, genuinely good curation.
  Free tier: 3 downloads/month, one credit line per track in the description.
  Paid tiers remove the credit and add channel safelisting against Content ID.
- **Free Music Archive** (freemusicarchive.org) — filter by CC-BY or CC0
  before falling in love with a track. CC-BY tracks are repo-safe with credit.

**When revenue justifies it:** Epidemic Sound or Artlist. Meaningfully better
curation than anything free, plus indemnification if a copyright claim lands.
This is what most pro launch films are actually scored with.

**Music shopping list for this repo's films:** one flagship corporate/cinematic
track (60-90s film), one upbeat 15s social track, one minimal ambient bed for
voiceover sections. Three great tracks beat thirty decent ones.

## Working rules

- Normalize finished mixes to about **-14 LUFS**.
- One sonic material per film: pick a whoosh family, a click family, one music
  track, and stay inside them. A CC0 grab-bag is the audio version of a
  mismatched font pairing.
- Re-pitch or layer the Kenney sounds slightly before shipping; they are the
  most-used free UI sounds in existence and people recognize them raw.
- Record the license next to every new file in LICENSES.md the day you add it.
