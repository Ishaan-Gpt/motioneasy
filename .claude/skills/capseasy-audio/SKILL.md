# CapsEasy audio sourcing

The full research lives at `assets/audio/SOURCES.md`. Per-file licenses at
`assets/audio/LICENSES.md`. Read SOURCES.md before downloading anything.

## The license rule (non-negotiable)

"Free for commercial use" does NOT mean safe to commit to this public repo.
Most free-audio licenses (Pixabay, Mixkit, Sonniss, ZapSplat) allow using the
sound **in renders** but forbid redistributing the **raw file**. Only CC0 and
CC-BY files may be committed here. Everything else lives on local disk and is
used in finished films only.

## What is already in the repo

- `assets/audio/sfx/kenney-interface-sounds/` + `kenney-ui-audio/` — 150 CC0
  UI sounds. Re-pitch or layer slightly before shipping; they are recognizable
  raw.
- `assets/audio/music/` — two CC-BY Kevin MacLeod tracks (Inspired,
  Funkorama). The credit line in LICENSES.md must appear in the video
  description or end card of any film using them.

## Where to get more

- SFX: Pixabay Sound Effects first, Freesound (filter CC0), Sonniss GDC
  bundle for pro quality. Mixkit and ZapSplat as backups.
- Music: Pixabay Music first, YouTube Audio Library, Mixkit Music, Uppbeat
  free tier (3/month, credit per track). Epidemic Sound / Artlist when
  revenue justifies it.

## Working rules

- Record the license next to every new file in LICENSES.md the day you add it.
- One sonic material per film: one whoosh family, one click family, one music
  track.
- Normalize finished mixes to about -14 LUFS.
